# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- Instant teleport: no loading screen. You are held in place while the area loads, then set on its ground with no fall damage.
- A hot reload of the mod no longer leaves its old map hook behind.

## 1.1.0

- Works on its own, with Jatify UI's look when that is installed.
- The menu is now a card in Jatify's design: the spot's coordinates and a Teleport here button with the portal icon.

## 1.0.0

- First release: admin right-click on the map, Teleport here.
